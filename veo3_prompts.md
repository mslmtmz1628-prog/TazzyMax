# Veo 3 Prompts: 43-second action-comedy sequence

6 clips (7+7+7+7+7+8 = 43 s). Prepend the CHARACTER BLOCK to every clip prompt.
Use each clip's last frame as the next clip's first frame (image-to-video) for continuity.

## CHARACTER BLOCK

```
Character A: early-20s man, short dark brown wavy hair, light stubble, plain light gray t-shirt, dark blue jeans, white sneakers.
Character B: 30s man, strong build, short black hair, long-sleeved black-and-white horizontally striped shirt, dark trousers, black boots.
Ultra-photorealistic cinematic action-comedy, handheld camera, 35mm lens, shallow natural depth of field, subtle film grain, natural skin texture, realistic physics and momentum. No slow motion. No text, subtitles, captions or watermarks.
```

## NEGATIVE PROMPT

```
cartoon, anime, distorted face, extra limbs, extra fingers, changing clothes, changing hairstyle, text, subtitles, captions, logo, watermark, slow motion, CGI look
```

## CLIP 1 (0-7 s): Mirror and grab

```
In a cramped, worn, abandoned apartment bathroom with cracked green-and-white tiles and a single warm yellow bulb, Character A stands at the mirror studying his face. Medium shot, handheld, slow push-in. In the mirror's reflection, Character B silently appears in the doorway behind him. A freezes, then B lunges and grabs him aggressively from behind. Audio: dripping tap, faint hum of the bulb, a sharp gasp, fabric rustle, a heavy thud as they collide with the sink. No dialogue, no music.
```

## CLIP 2 (7-14 s): Bathroom brawl

```
Continuing in the same tiny bathroom: A and B wrestle violently, shoving and pulling, stumbling against the sink and tiled walls, B tearing the shower curtain down. Fast handheld shots with light motion blur, close-ups of a hand gripping a collar, strained faces, sneakers slipping on wet tile. Warm practical light, natural shadows. Audio: grunts, scuffling shoes squeaking on tile, ceramic knocks, plastic curtain ripping, heavy breathing. No dialogue, no music.
```

## CLIP 3 (14-21 s): Wall break

```
In the same bathroom, B slams A back against a damaged plaster wall. The wall cracks and breaks through, opening a large hole into the adjoining room; dust and small debris fall naturally (one very brief slow-motion beat at impact only). A tumbles through, scrambles up and sprints away, B bursting out of the dust cloud behind him. Wide handheld shot then a fast tracking shot. Audio: loud crunch of breaking plaster, falling debris, coughing, pounding footsteps. No dialogue, no music.
```

## CLIP 4 (21-28 s): Chase and window

```
Inside the run-down apartment, A sprints down a narrow hallway, knocking over a chair and vaulting broken furniture over old newspapers on the floor, B chasing close behind, his hand grabbing at A's shirt and missing. Fast rear-follow tracking shot, low angle, then a close-up as A dives out of an open window. Audio: rapid footsteps, crashing furniture, panicked breathing, rustling cloth. No dialogue, no music.
```

## CLIP 5 (28-35 s): Fire escape

```
An old red-brick courtyard in warm late-afternoon light, wet ground. A lands on a rusty metal fire escape and scrambles up the stairs, B dropping into the courtyard and chasing from below. Wide establishing shot, then a close-up of A's breathless face. Rust flakes fall and the staircase shakes. B slips on a step, stumbles and crashes onto a dumpster lid. Audio: creaking rusty metal, clanging footsteps, heavy breathing, distant city ambience, a loud metallic bang at the end. No dialogue, no music.
```

## CLIP 6 (35-43 s, 8 s): Dumpster finale

```
Hard cut to a large outdoor dumpster full of cardboard and trash bags. Character A lies inside, exhausted and bewildered, holding a small green plush toy. Slow push-in toward his face as he looks around, glances at the plush, then raises one eyebrow toward the camera in a confused, deadpan reaction. Soft warm dusk light. Audio: muffled distant traffic, a bird, cardboard creaking as he shifts, one tired sigh, then a short comedic silence. No dialogue, no music.
```

## TIPS

- Generate reference images of A and B first; use "Ingredients to Video" / "Frames to Video" in Flow.
- If Veo adds subtitles, keep "No subtitles" in the prompt and use the negative prompt field.
- If the wall break or window jump looks wrong, cut away to dust or a face close-up.
- Use Veo 3 Fast for drafts, then regenerate keepers with standard Veo 3.
- Crossfade audio 0.3-0.5 s between clips when editing.
